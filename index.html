HTR-927

HTR-927 is a solvent-based contact adhesive for bonding laminates to particleboard and other substrates. It forms an instant, permanent bond, and the finished assembly can be routed, trimmed, and machined right away.

  • Instant, permanent bond with up to 30 minutes of open time once dry
  • Works on porous substrates, including CARB2 particleboard
  • Ready to route, trim, cut, and machine immediately after bonding
  • For professional and industrial use only
